In Canada, all legal cannabis must be regulated under the Cannabis Act, and must undergo safety and cannabinoid testing. Still, knowing how to read labels and lab results is essential for choosing safe, high-quality products.

Key Details to Be Aware Of

Look for the following information on licensed packaging:

  • THC and CBD Content (Potency)
  • Lot Number (helps verify source and testing)
  • Health Warning Label
  • Producer Information
  • Packaging Date
  • Terpene Profile (if available)
  • List of Ingredients
